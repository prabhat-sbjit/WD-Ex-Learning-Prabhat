Battlefield 2042 — Game Lore
The World of 2042

In the near future, the world has been ravaged by climate change, economic collapse, and geopolitical instability. Nation-states have crumbled, giving rise to No-Pats — stateless soldiers for hire with no allegiance to any flag.

The Flashpoint Crisis

Two superpowers — the United States and Russia — stand on the brink of all-out war. Both sides recruit No-Pat specialists to conduct deniable operations across the globe's most contested flashpoints.

The Specialists

Ten elite specialists, each with unique gadgets and abilities, are deployed across seven massive maps — from the frozen tundra of Antarctica to the towering skyscrapers of Singapore.
